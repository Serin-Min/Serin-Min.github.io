PIONA is an agent-based digital twin platform for human-centered mobility analysis. Agents are parameterized individually, so a heterogeneous population moves through the same space under different rules. The platform captures how those people interact with emerging mobility modes such as autonomous vehicles and mobile robots, and reports the resulting density, flow, and level of service.

I built the behavioral layer. The work spans agent typology, movement and response models, parameter optimization, calibration on observed trajectory and video data.
