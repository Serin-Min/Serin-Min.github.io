Projects

Agent-based simulation

MIMLAB

PIONA, agent-based digital twin

A pedestrian digital twin in which behavioral parameters vary across agents, preserving the population heterogeneity that aggregate models suppress. My contribution is the behavioral core, from agent taxonomy and route choice specification through parameter estimation and empirical calibration.

National Research Foundation of Korea (NRF) · 2024 to 2026

Generative AI digital twin for pedestrian activation

A generative AI framework for synthesizing pedestrian environments and the behavior they induce. Simulated response is coupled with observed demand, so candidate interventions can be appraised ex ante rather than evaluated after construction.

Architecture and Urban Research Institute (AURI) · 2026

Pedestrian behavior in super-aged environments

Microscopic behavior models estimated from living-lab and virtual reality trials with 113 participants. The work establishes how the built environment and emerging micromobility jointly condition walking speed, conflict exposure, and perceived stress among older pedestrians.

Architecture and Urban Research Institute (AURI) · 2026

Spatial LLM for citizen-informed planning Link

A relational representation of streets, buildings, parcels, and access points that grounds language-model reasoning in spatial structure. Planning alternatives are then evaluated against a distribution of simulated citizen responses rather than a single representative preference.

Travel demand modeling

The Seoul Institute and Seoul Metropolitan Government · 2025–2026

Pedestrian-oriented road space reallocation

Corridors prioritized through multi-criteria evaluation, then tested by network assignment in EMME and microscopic simulation in SUMO. The design isolates the network-wide displacement effects of reallocating vehicle capacity to pedestrian use.

Korea Development Institute (KDI) and Korea Research Institute for Local Administration (KRILA) · 2024–2026

Travel demand analysis for transport infrastructure investment

Travel demand forecasts and benefit-cost analysis prepared for preliminary feasibility studies and demand re-examinations of national road and rail investments. The work covers network coding, assignment, and the valuation of user benefits across alternative investment scenarios.

LG U+, Kakao, and NICE Zini Data · 2024–2025

Zone-level trip generation from pseudonymized data linkage

Mobile network and navigation records linked under pseudonymization to recover trip generation at fine zonal resolution. The approach supplies behaviorally grounded volume estimates where household travel surveys lose precision.

Korea Land and Housing Corporation (LH) · 2024

Network-based appraisal of urban development alternatives

Development alternatives for the Siheung Jeongwang public housing district, each evaluated through network-derived accessibility and pedestrian volume. Those measures entered the economic appraisal directly, placing scheme selection on quantitative rather than conventional grounds.

Sensing and autonomous mobility

MIMLAB and University of Seoul, TPL · 2026

Human-robot interaction in shared space

Shared-space trials between autonomous mobile robots and humans, with encounters characterized by minimum separation, time to collision, and post-encroachment time. Anticipatory stress in the seconds before each encounter, together with trip-level detour and delay, quantifies the cost humans bear where right of way is undefined.

Korea Agency for Infrastructure Technology Advancement (KAIA) · 2025–2026

High-precision positioning for autonomous vehicles

UAV-derived point clouds reduced to sparse object-based reference maps for vehicle localization under degraded GNSS. The formulation holds overbounding bias below 0.5 m while lowering computational load by 83 percent.

Korea and China joint research, Ministry of Science and ICT · 2025–2026

Traffic cyber-physical systems for vulnerable road users

Real-time conflict risk inferred from LiDAR and multispectral detection, spatio-temporal trajectory analysis, and V2X exchange between vehicles and roadside infrastructure. Risk estimates drive adaptive signal control and evacuation routing calibrated to the mobility constraints of vulnerable road users.

Climate and urban environment

2026

Thermal exposure and pedestrian route choice

Hourly thermal exposure joined with real-time de facto population to identify routes that minimize heat burden rather than distance alone. Urban Network Analysis from the MIT City Form Lab is paired with an agent-based model of my own, so that thermal stress and perceived comfort are resolved at the level of the individual traveler.

2024–2025

Vegetation dynamics under rail network expansion

Four decades of Landsat NDVI related to the timing of rail investment across metropolitan districts. Geographically and temporally weighted regression identifies where the association varies, with difference-in-differences supplying corroborating evidence.
