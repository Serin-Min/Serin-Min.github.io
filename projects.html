Projects

Agent-based simulation

MIMLAB

PIONA, agent-based digital twin

An agent-based digital twin in which every pedestrian carries its own behavioral parameters, so population heterogeneity is represented explicitly rather than averaged away. I developed the behavioral layer, covering agent typology, route choice and response models, parameter optimization, and calibration against observed trajectories.

National Research Foundation of Korea · 2024 to 2026

Generative AI digital twin for pedestrian activation

A digital twin of the walking environment driven by generative AI, built to test interventions intended to activate pedestrian movement. Simulated behavior is coupled with observed demand so that proposed changes can be appraised before implementation.

Architecture and Urban Research Institute · 2026

Pedestrian behavior in super-aged environments

Microscopic behavior models calibrated against living-lab and virtual reality trials with 113 participants. The study quantifies how physical barriers and emerging mobility modes alter walking speed, conflict frequency, and perceived stress in an ageing population.

Architecture and Urban Research Institute · 2026

Spatial LLM for citizen-informed planning

A relational graph of roads, buildings, parcels, and access points that grounds language-model reasoning in urban space. Large-scale agent deliberation over that graph allows planning alternatives to be evaluated against heterogeneous citizen responses rather than a single aggregate preference.

Travel demand modeling

The Seoul Institute · 2025 to 2026

Pedestrian-oriented road space reallocation

Candidate corridors ranked by entropy-weighted TOPSIS, followed by demand reassignment in EMME and microsimulation in SUMO. The analysis isolates the network-wide consequences of converting vehicle lanes to pedestrian space.

LG U+, Kakao, and NICE Zini Data

Zone-level trip generation from pseudonymized data linkage

Telecommunications and navigation records linked under pseudonymization to recover trip generation at fine zonal resolution. The linkage yields realistic volume estimates in settings where survey-based expansion factors break down.

Korea Development Institute and Korea Research Institute for Local Administration · 2024 to 2026

Travel demand analysis for transport infrastructure investment

Demand estimation and economic analysis supporting preliminary feasibility studies and demand re-examinations of national road and rail investments. The work spans network coding, assignment in EMME, and benefit estimation across planning scenarios.

Korea Land and Housing Corporation · 2024

Network-based appraisal of urban development alternatives

Alternative development schemes for the Siheung Jeongwang public housing district, each assessed through urban network analysis. Accessibility and pedestrian volumes derived from the network carried directly into the economic appraisal of every scheme.

Sensing and autonomous mobility

Korea Agency for Infrastructure Technology Advancement · 2025 to 2026

High-precision positioning for autonomous vehicles

UAV-derived point clouds compressed into sparse object-based reference maps for localization where GNSS degrades. The approach targets sub-0.5 m overbounding bias while cutting computational load by 83 percent.

Korea and China joint research, Ministry of Science and ICT · 2025 to 2026

Traffic cyber-physical systems for vulnerable road users

Real-time risk assessment built on LiDAR and multispectral object detection, spatio-temporal trajectory analysis, and V2X exchange between vehicles and roadside infrastructure. The system derives adaptive signal control and evacuation routing matched to the mobility constraints of vulnerable road users.

MIMLAB and University of Seoul

Robot and pedestrian interaction in shared space

Mixed-traffic trials between autonomous mobile robots and pedestrians, with interaction episodes characterized by minimum separation, time to collision, and post-encounter time. Trip-level detour and delay quantify the cost each party absorbs.

Climate and urban environment

Seoul

Thermal exposure and pedestrian route choice

Hourly heat exposure combined with real-time de facto population to identify least-exposed routes rather than merely shortest ones. Urban Network Analysis, developed at the MIT City Form Lab, is paired with an agent-based model of my own so that travel stress and thermal comfort are evaluated for individual travelers.

Seoul Metropolitan Area

Vegetation dynamics under rail network expansion

Four decades of Landsat NDVI across metropolitan districts related to the timing of rail investment, using geographically and temporally weighted regression with difference-in-differences as corroborating evidence.
